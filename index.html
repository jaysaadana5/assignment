Selected work

Exploratory Data Analysis · Urban Mobility

Optimising NYC Yellow Taxi Operations

An end-to-end EDA on NYC Yellow Taxi trip records — cleaning and validating raw data, then analysing temporal demand, zone-level activity, and fare structure to produce actionable routing, positioning, and pricing strategy.

Routing & Dispatch

Demand peaks 15:00–20:00 with a morning commuter hump. Recommended dynamic dispatch to high-pickup zones and rerouting away from congested Midtown corridors at rush hour.

Zone Positioning

Choropleth and pickup/dropoff-ratio analysis surfaced "taxi graveyard" sink zones and airport hotspots (JFK, LaGuardia) — driving rebalancing and staging recommendations.

Pricing Strategy

Fare-per-mile falls with distance; late-night trips attract above-average tips. Surcharge patterns by zone and hour revealed clear tariff-optimisation opportunities.
